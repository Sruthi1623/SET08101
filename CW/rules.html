🧾 How to Play

🎯 Mission Objective

You are Agent Cipher of the Global Intelligence Agency.

Your goal: infiltrate The Cryptix and stop the cyber-attack by solving encrypted puzzles.

🧩 Cipher Types

  • Caesar Cipher: A letter shift (e.g., A → D).
  • Morse Code: Dots & dashes patterns.
  • Base64: Computer-friendly encoding.
  • Double Encryption: Base64 ▶ Caesar combo.

💡 Hints

Each level allows 3 free hints. After that, hints cost 10 coins.

🪙 Coins & Score

  • Start with 100 coins.
  • -5 coins for each hint used.
  • -1 coin per 5 seconds taken.
  • More coins = higher final rank.

🧳 Shop & Tools

Use your coins in the Spy Shop to unlock:

  • 🧠 Extra Hints
  • 🔓 Decryption Keys
  • 🕒 Time Extensions (coming soon)

🏁 How to Win

Complete all missions, solve the final cipher, trace the signal, and save the world.
