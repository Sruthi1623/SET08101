🧾 How to Play

🎯 Mission Objective

You are Agent Cipher of the Global Intelligence Agency.

Your mission: infiltrate The Cryptix Network and decode all intercepted transmissions before time runs out.

🧩 Cipher Types

  • Caesar Cipher: A classic letter shift (e.g., A → D).
  • Morse Code: Dots and dashes patterns representing letters.
  • Base64: Computer encoding often ending with "=" signs.
  • Double Encryption: Two-step ciphers (e.g., Base64 ➔ Caesar).

💡 Hints

  • Each level offers 3 free hints.
  • After free hints, each extra hint costs 10 coins.

🪙 Coins & Scoring

  • Start with 100 base coins at the beginning.
  • Earn bonus coins based on speed and accuracy in each level.
  • -5 coins penalty per hint used.
  • -1 coin penalty every 5 seconds taken.

🛒 Spy Shop

  • 🧠 Unlock extra hints if you run out.
  • 🔓 Access decryption tips (coming soon).

🏁 Winning Conditions

Complete all decoding challenges, manage your coins wisely, and achieve the highest agent rank possible.
